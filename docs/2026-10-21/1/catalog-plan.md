# The Technologies list: groups you can move between, and "the catalog" (21 Oct)

From the user's four points on the Technologies list, signed off 21 Oct (all four choices the recommended ones).

## What the groups are today

- **Emerging / Mature**: the technology's commercial availability. Research, Pilot and Early commercial count as
  emerging; Mature is mature; Unclassified is neither.
- **"Tata's list"**: the file Tata sent, *Emerging-technologies October 2026.xlsx* (967 technologies: 885 added,
  82 matched to ones the radar had found). "New to Tata" = emerging and not in it; "Mature, not on Tata's list" =
  mature and not in it.
- **Possible nascent**: the model flagged it from a page; a person says Yes or No.
- **Little or no evidence**: in the file, but thin evidence or no pages.

None of this was said on the page, and nothing could put a technology into the file or take it out.

## Build

1. **Move between groups** — the bar shown when rows are ticked gets one "Move to…" menu:
   - Commercial availability: Research, Pilot, Early commercial, Mature, Unclassified (Emerging ↔ Mature).
   - Catalog: Add to the catalog / Take out of the catalog — a new person's choice (`catalog`, yes | no), with
     who, when and Undo; it wins over the import, and a later import doesn't undo it.
   - Possibly nascent: Yes / No for every ticked row; refused, with the reason, for one the model hasn't flagged.
   - "Move domain to…" stays beside it. Thin evidence can't be moved by hand: it leaves once Find evidence finds
     pages (said in the note).
   - The technology's own page: "In the catalog (file, date)" or "Not in the catalog", with Add / Take out and Undo.
2. **Say which list** — a visible "How the groups work" note under the group buttons: each group's rule in one
   line, the catalog's file, date and count, and how to move technologies.
3. **Rows** — commercial availability and domain become plain text with where they came from and Undo; no menus.
   One technology: tick it and use the bar.
4. **"Tata's list" → "catalog"** wherever a person reads it (list, technology page, Import, Domains, answer cards,
   the Point of View's display, the filter "In the catalog or not"). Group buttons, short deck words:
   Emerging · New to Tata · Mature · Blind spots · Possibly nascent · In catalog, thin evidence.
   The Point of View skill says "from Tata's catalog" (pov v8); the 23 Points of View that say "Tata's list" are
   rewritten. Internal names follow (`in_catalog`, filter `catalog`); view ids stay (they're links).
